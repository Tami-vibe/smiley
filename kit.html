#faq[data-look="b"]
Do I have to pay upfront if the accident is covered by insurance? expand_more
No. We have direct billing agreements with all major insurance carriers (Allianz, Generali, UnipolSai, AXA). Once you provide the claim number, we handle the settlement directly with no upfront payment from you.
How long will the repair take, and do you offer a replacement vehicle? expand_more
Minor bodywork takes 2-3 business days. Heavy collision or unibody repairs take 5-8 business days. We provide a courtesy replacement car free of charge for the entire duration of certified repairs.
Is your paint finish covered under guarantee? expand_more
Yes. All paintwork carried out in our sealed downdraft booth carries a full lifetime workmanship warranty against peeling, cracking, or color fading.
