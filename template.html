    FREQUENT QUESTIONS

    Quick answers.

    Everything you need to know about collision repair, insurance claims, rental cars, and factory warranties.

    Call WorkshopWhatsApp Dispatch
    Do I have to use the body shop my insurer suggests?
    No. By Italian law, you have the right to choose your preferred certified body shop. We manage the claim directly with your insurer.
    How long does a repair take?
    Most repairs take a few working days. We confirm the exact timeline with your initial quote.
    Do you offer a courtesy car?
    Yes, ask us when you book. Courtesy vehicles are available for the entire duration of your repair.
    Can you match my car’s original paint?
    Yes. We use spectrophotometer computer colour matching and factory clearcoats.
    How do I get a quote?
    Send photos with the form above or by WhatsApp. We reply within 24 hours.
