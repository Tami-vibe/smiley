Recipe #06

FAQ: Questions & Clear Answers

id="faq" class="ks ks-faq"
Questions Drivers Ask

Clear Answers Before Any Key Hand-off

Do I have to use the repair shop chosen by my insurer? expand_more

No. Under Italian and EU antitrust regulations, you have the legal right to choose any certified repair shop. We handle the paperwork directly with your insurer so you don’t pay out of pocket.

How do you guarantee the paint will match? expand_more

We use an optical spectrophotometer to scan the current fade level of your car’s actual clearcoat, then mix the exact tint formula in-house rather than using a standard tin.

What warranty comes with the repair? expand_more

All structural frame realignment and booth paint handoffs carry our full 5-year written craftsmanship warranty.
